The AI bill says $0.05.
The finished task costs $2.15.

That is what happens in the worked example in our new VorkLab guide once you include the work around the model: checking the draft, fixing mistakes, handling exceptions, maintaining the workflow and paying for setup.

At 1,000 enquiries a month, the example comes out at:

• $3.00 per result with manual handling
• $2.15 with AI drafts reviewed by a person
• $2.32 with more autonomous processing

The last option needs less human handling. It still costs more than reviewed drafts at this volume because setup and support have to be paid for too.

Change the volume, and the ranking changes.
Add just one minute of review to every draft, and the monthly cost rises by $500. That is ten times the model-and-tool bill in this example.

These are illustrative planning numbers, not client results. The article shows every assumption and includes an interactive calculator and a worksheet for your own workflow.

Before automating a task, count what it takes to finish it.
